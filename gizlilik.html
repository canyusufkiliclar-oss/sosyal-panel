Who uses this app

Sosyal Panel is a personal tool used only by its developer to manage his own social media accounts. It is not available to the public.

What data is accessed

When the developer connects one of his own accounts, the app receives an access token from the platform (YouTube/Google, TikTok, Meta/Facebook/Instagram, X). Using this token it reads basic profile information (name, username, profile picture, follower and video counts, recent posts and their view/like counts) and uploads videos that the developer selects.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the developer's own computer. Access tokens and app secrets are stored in the macOS Keychain. Upload history and settings are stored in local files. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official APIs of the platforms themselves.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its token from the Keychain. Access can also be revoked at any time from each platform's own account settings.

Türkçe özet

Uygulama yalnızca geliştiricinin kendi hesaplarına erişir. Oturum anahtarları macOS Anahtar Zinciri'nde, geçmiş ve ayarlar yerel dosyalarda tutulur. Veriler platformların resmi API'leri dışında hiçbir yere gönderilmez, satılmaz ve paylaşılmaz.
